# Realtime Voice Translation

Realtime translation is more than speech recognition.

The entire voice path has to stay synchronized.

## Typical architecture

Caller

↓

PBX / SBC

↓

Realtime AI

↓

Translation

↓

Human operator

## Key components

- Low latency audio
- Language detection
- Streaming translation
- Context preservation
- Seamless transfer

## Lesson

Good translation starts with good telephony.

Poor audio quality cannot be fixed by AI.
